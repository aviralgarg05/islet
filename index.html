Casement

Approve your coding agent's permission requests from the MacBook notch.

Claude Code, Codex and Cursor ask in the notch. One click, and they carry on. Your music, your iPhone's Live Activities and your timers live there too.

Download for macOS Source on GitHub

Free and MIT licensed. macOS 14 or later, Intel and Apple silicon. No account, no telemetry, nothing leaves your Mac.

What else is in there

  • Now PlayingFrom any app, browsers included, with synced lyrics, a scrubber that seeks and an output picker.
  • Your iPhone's Live ActivitiesRides, deliveries, scores and flights in the notch instead of behind it, with 137 apps' own looks. How much shows depends on what the app exposes; Casement leaves Apple's own pill alone rather than covering it with less.
  • Meetings, timers and remindersA meeting counts down beside the notch and waits with a Join button.
  • A shelf, clipboard history and downloadsDrop files on the notch, AirDrop one in a click, find the link you copied an hour ago.
  • Twelve tools, each off until you want itTo-dos, a note, a converter, emoji, weather, a camera mirror, a teleprompter, stocks and more.
  • Your own activitiesFrom a script, the command line, an HTTP API, the casement:// scheme or an iPhone Shortcut.

First launch

Builds are not notarised by Apple yet, so macOS blocks the first launch. Once:

  1. Open Casement. macOS says it cannot verify it. Close the message, without choosing Move to Trash.
  2. Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security, and scroll to Security.
  3. Next to the message that Casement was blocked, click Open Anyway.
  4. Click Open Anyway again, and use your password or Touch ID.

On macOS 15 and later, Control-clicking the app no longer gets past this check.
